pyhealth.models.ETHOS
===================================

ETHOS: a GPT-style generative model of tokenized patient timelines, with
zero-shot risk estimation by sampling future timelines
(`Renc et al., 2025 <https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06124>`_). Train it on
:class:`~pyhealth.tasks.EthosTimelineMEDS`, then estimate risks on
:class:`~pyhealth.tasks.EthosOutcomeMEDS`.
