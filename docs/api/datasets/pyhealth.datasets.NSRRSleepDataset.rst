pyhealth.datasets.NSRRSleepDataset
===================================

Overnight polysomnography recordings and expert-scored sleep stages from the
National Sleep Research Resource (NSRR): the Sleep Heart Health Study (SHHS) and
the MESA sleep exam. Access requires an approved data-use request, see
`SHHS <https://sleepdata.org/datasets/shhs>`_ and
`MESA <https://sleepdata.org/datasets/mesa>`_.
